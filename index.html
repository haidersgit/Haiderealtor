I grew up in Jersey City, in a house across the street from Ferris High School. That block is where I learned what a home actually does to a family.

On our street, some families rented and some owned. The ones who owned treated their homes differently. They planted gardens, fixed up their porches, and stayed for years. Owning a home gave them roots, and that stuck with me.

I spent a lot of my free time back then volunteering around the neighborhood. The part I liked most is the same part I like now: talking to people, figuring out what they actually need, and leaving the block a little better than I found it. I didn't know it yet, but that was the job.

Then we moved to Bayonne, and I spent the next ten years there. It's where I opened my first licensing book and decided I wanted to be the one handing people that deed. Nine years later I'm still licensed in the state I grew up in, and I've lived in nearly every corner of it.

Today I'm based in South Jersey and I sell more homes in Lawnside than anyone else. I'll still drive to Jersey City for you. I know what the walk from the front door to the curb feels like in every part of this state.
